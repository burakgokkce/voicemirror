Privacy Policy

VoiceMirror: Speech Coach · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what VoiceMirror ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: burakgokkce@icloud.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple or Google we receive a user ID and your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address), and optionally your name.
  • Recordings: the audio you choose to record, stored in a private storage area that only your account can access.
  • Transcripts and reports: the text of your recording with word timings, and the measurements calculated from it (pace, filler words, pauses, repetitions, score).
  • Preferences: your goal, language and how long audio should be kept.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe, Apple-signed transaction details (product, dates, status) so we can unlock Pro. We never see your payment details.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

How speech is processed

To create a transcript, the app sends your recording to Apple's speech recognition service through iOS. Apple processes it under Apple's Privacy Policy. The measurements and suggestions are then calculated in the app from that transcript.

Why we use it

Only to provide the app: show your reports and history, apply the free weekly limit, unlock Pro, and keep your account secure.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (database and private file storage), protected by row-level access rules so each account can only read its own data. Data may be processed outside your country.

How long we keep it

  • Audio is deleted automatically after the period you choose in the app (7 days by default), or immediately when you delete a recording.
  • Transcripts, reports and your account stay until you delete them or your account.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any recording from its report, and delete your account and all associated data in the app under Profile > Delete account. Microphone and speech recognition permissions can be turned off in iOS Settings. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data; depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK) you may have additional rights.

Children

VoiceMirror is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
