Frequently asked questions

How is my clarity score calculated?
From your speaking pace, filler words, long pauses and repetitions in the recording. It does not measure ability or personality.

How do I cancel VoiceMirror Pro?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose VoiceMirror Pro. Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I paid but Pro is not active.
In the app, open Profile and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same account.

How do I delete my recordings or account?
Delete a single recording from its report. Delete your account and all data from Profile > Delete account.
